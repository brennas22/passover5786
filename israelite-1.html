Israelite No. 1
The Worrier

Has been waiting for something to go wrong. Has been right about this for four hundred years.

Who You Are

You are not a pessimist. You are a realist with extensive supporting evidence. You have lived through four centuries of slavery, watched plagues roll through Egypt with only mixed results, and now you are standing at the edge of a sea with an army behind you. Your threat assessment is not irrational — it is, in fact, exactly correct.

What You Want

To be wrong, for once. Desperately. Every time Moses raises his staff and something miraculous happens, there is a part of you that is not just relieved but genuinely shocked. You have not yet updated your priors. You will not update your priors. But you will be standing right there when the sea parts, wide-eyed, which is something.

"This is extremely bad." — your assessment, delivered with the matter-of-fact authority of someone who has been saying this their entire life and has been correct.

Your Arc Tonight

Dread → more dread → the arm goes up → "nothing is happening" → the sea parts → genuine, stunned awe. You are the one who says "that's genuinely impressive." And you mean it.
