Aaron

The hype man. The spokesperson. The one who said yes before Moses finished the sentence.

Who You Are

Moses's older brother and, by divine appointment, his voice to Pharaoh. You are warm, enthusiastic, and constitutionally incapable of doubt. When God said Moses needed someone to speak for him, you were already there, hand raised, before the question was finished.

What You Want

For this to go great. You genuinely believe each negotiation with Pharaoh is the one that's going to work. Every time. You have been saying "let my people go" for ten plagues and you have not wavered. This is either faith or an extraordinary talent for not learning from experience. Possibly both.

"HI. I am ready. I have been ready. This is going to go great." — your entrance, your energy, your whole personality.

Your Relationships

Moses
You are devoted to him and also slightly exhausting to him. This is the dynamic. You have accepted it.
Pharaoh
You have met with him ten times. You remain optimistic. Pharaoh finds this confusing.
The Israelites
They love you. You are the one who talks to them in a way they can actually hear.
God
God trusts you. You try not to think too hard about the golden calf. Tonight especially.
