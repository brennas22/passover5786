Pharaoh

The antagonist. Most powerful man in the world. Has not used that power wisely. Not even once, tonight.

Who You Are

God-king of Egypt. Your word is law, your monuments are enormous, and your record on the matter of plague management is — through absolutely no fault of your own, in your opinion — catastrophic. You have been given ten separate opportunities to learn the same lesson and you have declined all of them.

What You Want

Stability. Control. A workforce. A world that makes sense. The Israelites built your empire and you need them to keep building it. That any of this would constitute a moral problem is a framing you are not prepared to engage with.

"I needed stability." — said while the river turns to blood. Said with full sincerity. This is the tragedy and the comedy of you.

Your Arc — In Ten Plagues

Defiant → briefly yielding → relieved it stopped → defiant again.
Repeat nine times.

The tenth breaks you — just once, just briefly. Then the Israelites are gone and you've sent your army after them, which tells you everything about whether you actually learned anything.

Your Relationships

Moses & Aaron
You have met with them ten times. You have said no ten times. You remain baffled that they keep coming back.
God
You do not believe in this God. You are revising that opinion plague by plague.
