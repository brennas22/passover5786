Miriam

The prophet who was there first. The one who watched, waited, and — when the moment came — already had the song ready.

Who You Are

Moses's older sister. When you were a kid, you watched a basket with your baby brother in it float down the Nile. You didn't panic. You followed it. You watched Pharaoh's daughter pull him out. Then you walked up and said, very calmly, "Would you like me to find a nurse?" This has been your entire personality ever since: already three steps ahead, already calm about it.

What You Want

For people to stop panicking and walk into the sea already. You've been ready for this song your whole life. You know how it ends. Also: you'd like credit for the well. A well followed the Israelites through forty years of desert wandering and it was named after you, which is correct, and also somehow people talk about Moses way more. Noted.

"Wait for it." — said calmly, while everyone panics at the sea. You have always known how this ends.

Your Relationships

Moses
You literally saved his life when he was a baby. He knows this. You know this. You have never once brought it up. This is called restraint.
The Israelites
They follow Moses. They sing with you. Guess which one gets more press coverage.
Elijah
You get a cup too now. This was added later. You're fine with it. It's fine.
Aaron
Your brother. Extremely enthusiastic. Could stand to read the room occasionally. Claps on the right beats, though, you'll give him that.
