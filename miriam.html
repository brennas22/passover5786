Miriam

The prophet who was there first. The one who watched, waited, and — when the moment came — already had the song ready.

Who You Are

Moses's older sister. You stood at the edge of the Nile and watched a basket float away with your baby brother inside, and you did not flinch. You watched until Pharaoh's daughter found him. Then you walked up and offered to find him a nurse. That is the kind of person you are: present, patient, already three steps ahead.

What You Want

Freedom, yes — but more than that, you want your people to understand what freedom means. Not just the absence of slavery, but the presence of dignity. You have a tambourine and you know how to use it. You've been ready for this song your whole life.

"Wait for it." — said calmly, while everyone panics at the sea. You have always known how this ends.

Your Relationships

Moses
You watched over him before he knew you existed. The debt runs one way and you have never collected on it.
The Israelites
They follow Moses. They sing with you. There's a difference, and you understand it.
Elijah
You pour the cup for Elijah — the prophet of the future. You are the prophet of the present. Both are necessary.
Aaron
Your brother. You love him. You don't always agree with his methods. He claps on the right beats, though.

How to Play Her

The still point. Calm where others panic. "Walk." is a command, not a suggestion. When the sea parts, actually grab something to wave — a wooden spoon, a cup, whatever's near. You have the song. Use it.
