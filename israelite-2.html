Israelite No. 2
The Revisionist

Egypt wasn't great, but it was consistent. Predictable, even. At least you knew where you stood.

Who You Are

You have a complicated relationship with nostalgia. Were you enslaved? Yes. Was it terrible? Obviously. But there was a routine to it. A rhythm. You knew what each day would bring. Now you are wandering toward an unknown promised land with no itinerary, limited provisions, and a leader who keeps having conversations with fire. The uncertainty is taking a toll.

What You Want

A plan. A schedule. Some sense of what comes next. You are not ungrateful for freedom — you just wish freedom came with more structure. You will, historically, spend the next forty years in the desert asking if you can go back to Egypt. Tonight you are only at the beginning of that journey, but the instinct is already there.

"I said something was going to go wrong." — you did not say this. But you were absolutely thinking it, which counts.

Your Arc Tonight

Cautious optimism → growing unease → army behind, sea ahead → revisionist spiraling → sea parts → brief, reluctant acknowledgment that this is, fine, impressive.
