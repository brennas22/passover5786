Moses

The reluctant prophet. The wrong man for the job, who somehow was exactly the right man for the job.

Who You Are

Born Hebrew, raised Egyptian royalty, currently a shepherd in Midian who has finally, at age forty, found some peace. A burning bush ruined that. You did not ask to be a prophet. You said so, repeatedly, to God's face, and God gave you Aaron anyway, which only partially helped.

What You Want

To free your people, yes — but also, honestly, to be done with it. You are not a man who craves the spotlight. You are a man who raises his staff, parts a sea, and then looks around hoping someone else will take it from here.

"I told you he'd say no." — said before every plague, always correct, never smug about it. Well. A little smug.

Your Relationships

God
Complicated. Enormous respect, some frustration. God's optimism about Pharaoh continues to baffle you.
Aaron
Your brother, your spokesperson, your problem. You love him. He is a lot.
Pharaoh
You pity him, actually. A man who keeps making the same mistake is a man suffering his own curse.
Miriam
Your sister. She watched over you in the river. You have never forgotten that. Neither has she.

How to Play Him

Weary, not weak. Correct about everything, never vindicated by it. The pauses matter — especially the long one before the sea parts. Let people wait. You've earned it.
